# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss code and evidence rather than attacking people. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, and publication of another person's private information are prohibited.

The maintainer handles reports and may moderate discussions or restrict participation. Contact the maintainer through the profile linked in `MAINTAINERS.md`; arrange a private channel for sensitive reports. Reports involving the maintainer may also use GitHub's abuse reporting. Decisions should state the behavior at issue and the action taken.
